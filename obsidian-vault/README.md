# Obsidian Vault for Cindy

Deeply connect your local Obsidian vaults to Cindy—inspired by OpenAI MCP and Gemini CLI workflows for structured second-brain reasoning.

## Highlights

- **Composer `@` Mention**: Type `@` in the chat box to search notes by title, folder, or `#tag` and insert `[[Wikilinks]]` instantly.
- **Graph & Metadata Aware**: Parses YAML Frontmatter properties, nested tags, outgoing `[[wikilinks]]`, backlinks, unlinked mentions, two-hop neighbors, and `.canvas` boards.
- **Safe Section-Level Editing**: Append, prepend, or replace content under a specific Markdown heading, merge frontmatter keys safely, and require explicit confirmation before overwriting or moving notes to `.trash`.
- **Native Workbench & One-Click Capture**: Save any AI response directly from the message menu, browse notes in the sidebar/popover workbench, or jot into today's Daily Note.
- **Bundled Slash Skills**: Includes `/obsidian-capture` and `/obsidian-daily`.
